Problems I'll Solve

5 real challenges. 5 concrete answers.

01

No unified partner dataset across the merged organizations

BD and the combined entity now run two parallel CRMs, partner portals, and sales hierarchies. The merger created a data fragmentation crisis — you cannot cross-sell what you cannot see.

I migrated 5,000+ customer records from legacy systems into Snowflake at Choice Solutions, establishing centralized governance that cut cross-team reporting errors by 40%. I'll apply the same playbook here.
02

Partner selection criteria are subjective and inconsistent

Without a scoring framework, sales teams rely on gut feel to choose channel partners — leading to underperforming relationships in emerging markets and missed cross-sell opportunities.

I'll build a quantitative partner scoring model in Python/SQL incorporating revenue potential, market coverage, product portfolio alignment, and competitive overlap — making selection defensible and repeatable.
03

Stakeholders across sales, marketing, and finance speak different data dialects

KPI definitions diverge between teams (e.g., "installed base" means something different in instruments vs. diagnostics), causing alignment delays before every QBR.

At Choice Solutions, I led cross-functional KPI standardization that reduced alignment time by 50%. I'll replicate this — documenting agreed definitions and embedding them directly into the dashboard layer.
04

No real-time visibility into channel coverage gaps by geography or product line

Without a coverage heatmap, commercial leaders cannot see which emerging markets lack representation or where the two commercial territories overlap, wasting both partners' capacity.

I'll build Power BI or Tableau dashboards visualizing coverage density by region, product category, and partner type — giving leadership a weekly "coverage score" they can act on immediately.
05

Cross-sell model lacks the analytical foundation to reach $290M synergy target

Revenue synergies only materialize if the right diagnostics customers are matched to the right instruments portfolio — and vice versa. That matching requires ML-assisted affinity modeling on the installed base.

I built regression models predicting Super Bowl ad engagement with 82% accuracy and DCF valuation models for a $144B market. I'll apply predictive modeling techniques to customer affinity scoring that directly feeds the cross-sell pipeline.
